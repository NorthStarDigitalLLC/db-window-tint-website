BEFORE YOU STOP BY

GOOD
TO KNOW.

How do I get a price for my vehicle?

Call (864) 556-2700 with your vehicle’s year, make, and model. For tint, mention the windows you want done. For audio, describe your current system and what you would like to change. The shop can confirm options and pricing.

Which tint should I choose?

Start with your priorities: appearance, privacy, or comfort. Ask the shop to walk through its available films, shades, and what is appropriate for your vehicle before deciding.

Can I discuss tint and audio together?

Yes—select “Both” in the message builder, or bring up both projects when you call. Confirm equipment, scheduling, and the full quote directly with the shop.

Should I call before visiting?

Please call to confirm current hours and appointment availability before driving over. The shop is at 1001 Wiles Road in Anderson.
